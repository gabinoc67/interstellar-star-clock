A · Atmosphere and assumed gravity

ReadingValueSource type
The 80 m boundary is an assigned pressure contour, not a vacuum seal. This local calculation assumes a hypothetical force normal to every hull surface, plus a prescribed hull pressure. Air escape, disturbances, radiation shielding, and global atmospheric stability are not solved. The intense outer gravity zone would be hazardous to people and equipment.

B · Magnetic field and fission power

ReadingValueSource type
The winding is an ideal reference solenoid used to estimate electrical demand. Its calculated central field is not proof of minimum field strength around an irregular hull. Full three-dimensional field geometry, structural loads, fringe energy and shielding efficiency remain unsolved. Plasma is an electrically powered medium, not a separate energy source.

Mission and external gravity

ReadingValueSource type
Fixed inputs, equations and limitations

Changing the simulation clock updates derived readings automatically. Constant values remain constant because their design inputs do not change. There are no parameter adjusters.

Earth, Sun and Pluto are fixed on a radial line; other bodies and orbital motion are omitted. Cruise is prescribed at 100 km/s. Rear plasma thrust is calculated separately; it is not integrated into the prescribed trajectory. Launch, acceleration and braking are excluded. Constant density, resistivity and ideal heat rejection are reference approximations.
A normal central mass creates a field directed toward its center; a force following every irregular hull surface would need a different, unestablished mechanism. A liquid-metal dynamo can generate magnetism under suitable conditions, but does not supply this artificial gravity.
Shielding model: one proton incident perpendicular to a locally uniform field. A 90° turn within the layer is a local trajectory criterion, not a whole-ship protection claim. Photons, lasers, gamma rays and neutral meteoroids are not magnetically deflected in this model.
Air pressure below the 80 m pressure contour is estimated in a local isothermal approximation. The atmosphere does not abruptly stop there. A global escape and confinement solution, artificial-gravity generation, its power cost and safe operation are all unresolved.
