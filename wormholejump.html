Hypothesis, equations, constants and references

Scope: The animated passage assumes ds²=−c²dt²+dℓ²+(ℓ²+r₀²)dΩ². The ordinary Einstein–Rosen bridge is nontraversable; the animation uses a separate traversable toy geometry. Formation, stability and a physical magnetic-to-wormhole coupling are outside this calculation. Positive magnetic energy alone does not supply this geometry's required negative energy. Source residuals below quantify that mismatch. Speed and curvature do not uniquely determine a reactor-to-curvature mechanism.

Hybrid: Dry mass 190 million kg, initial propellant 100 million kg; initial velocity and exhaust speed are adjustable. Requested acceleration is 0.0001 m/s²; maximum engine electrical input 3,200 MW, jet efficiency 60%. F=min(a_requested m,2ηP_engine/ve); mass flow F/ve. Each step uses Δv=±ve ln(m_before/m_after), with braking after ℓ=0. Newtonian rocket dynamics are used at the permitted speeds; proper time and kinetic energy include special-relativistic diagnostics. No launch, orbit or gravity from planets is included. The ultrastatic metric has no static radial gravitational acceleration.

Dipole: Axis is transverse (+y) to flight (+x). Outside ship radius R, B_eq(r)=B_eq(R)(R/r)³; pole field is twice the equatorial field. External dipole field energy U=4πB_eq²R³/(3μ₀). Coil/core energy is omitted. Field target B_target=B_base sqrt(v/v_initial) (1+0.5 exp(−(ℓ/r₀)²)); this is an explicit controller assumption. Charging is limited to 600 MW minus plasma input, with loss λU, λ=0.001/s. Releasing excess field energy is recorded as dumped heat. Equal front/rear sample fields follow dipole symmetry, rather than invented directional asymmetry.

Lorentz: Ship-frame electric field is set to zero. An incident plasma particle has relative speed v_ship+400 km/s; the selected equatorial trajectory is perpendicular to B. |F|=|q|v_relative B; gyroradius=γmv_relative/(|q|B). Incident particles are local samples, not integrated trajectories. v_relative B is a motional electric-field scale, not an additional ship-frame E force. q=0 gives zero direct Lorentz force. Neutral interactions and radiation shielding are separate engineering questions.

Casimir: a=a_center[1−0.25 exp(−(ℓ/r₀)²)] sqrt(v_initial/v_ship). This quasistatic controller is hypothetical; the ideal plate formulas are E=−π²ℏcA/(720a³), u=E/(Aa), p_normal=3u, p_tangent=−u. Gap power is dE/dt=−F da/dt at fixed area. Actuator electrical efficiency and plate inertia are omitted; reversible energy exchange is displayed separately. Plate separation is bounded to 10–20,000 nm. Surface corrections, finite temperature and material conductivity are omitted. Internal attraction has zero whole-ship thrust.

Einstein source: With r=√(ℓ²+r₀²), K=c⁴r₀²/(8πGr⁴). Required local orthonormal source: u_req=−K, p_x_req=−K, p_y_req=p_z_req=+K. Radial tension=K; radial NEC=u_req+p_x_req=−2K. Ricci scalar=−2r₀²/r⁴. A radially moving geodesic observer has transverse tidal coefficient γ²v²r₀²/r⁴; a 2 m separation gives the displayed stretching acceleration. Radial geodesic tides are zero in this metric. Rocket acceleration is a separate load.

Local comparison: At the equatorial reference point B is along y: u_B=B²/(2μ₀), p_x_B=+u_B, p_y_B=−u_B, p_z_B=+u_B. Plates have normal x: p_x_C=3u_C, p_y_C=p_z_C=−u_C. Residuals T_req−T_B−T_C are the additional source required if these idealized local contributions are assigned together. This is a local stress comparison, not a global self-consistent spacetime solution. Coil stresses and supporting plate hardware are omitted. A density ratio or field-energy mass term alone does not establish gravitational propulsion.

Power: Reactor thermal capacity 10,000 MW with 40% conversion, ship systems 20 MW. The source-energy residual compares reactor output against heat, jet energy, escaping layer energy and stored field/layer energy. Engine waste, conversion losses, ship-system use and released magnetic energy enter heat. The total energy required to create/maintain the assumed geometry is not inferred from this onboard budget.

Layer: Hydrogen p/e shell from R to 2R; supply target 10¹² pairs/s times incident-speed ratio and [1+0.5 exp(−(ℓ/r₀)²)]. Residence time 1 s, temperature 100,000 K; pair energy includes 13.6 eV ionization plus 3kT. Ambient comparison density 5 protons/cm³. Inventory updates with dN/dt=supply−N/τ. Beta is a pressure comparison, not proof of confinement.
