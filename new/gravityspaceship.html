SCIENTIFIC DISCLAIMER
This simulator combines established equations with assumed vehicle parameters and a separate hypothetical gravity/spacetime term.

The animated baseline trajectory uses aΦ = 0. The aΦ = 2.00 m/s² case is an unverified comparison only.

No experimentally demonstrated mechanism is assumed to produce aΦ from fission, plasma, magnetic fields, or any other system.

The Einstein inverse equation describes a mathematical target-source relationship. It does not prove that the required stress-energy tensor can be physically produced.

This is a 1-D classical research simulator, not a complete orbital, CFD, reactor, plasma, or general-relativity solver.
