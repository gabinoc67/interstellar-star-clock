Three-Field Hypothesis

Front Gravity: The larger forward field represents the hypothetical expanded/deformed region of spacetime.

Middle Gravity: The spacecraft remains within a central gravity-reference environment intended to provide a smoother local region for the vehicle and crew.

Rear Gravity: A smaller compressed region forms behind the craft as the vehicle progresses forward.

The simulation automatically changes all three fields after the START button is pressed.

Crew-Safe Motion Hypothesis

The purpose of this model is not primarily to make the spacecraft travel faster than light.

Instead, the hypothesis asks whether controlled spacetime geometry could reduce the effective travel path while the crew remains within a comparatively smooth gravitational reference environment.

atidal ≈ Δg / Δx

Large gravitational differences across a vehicle would produce greater tidal effects. Therefore the model attempts to change the field gradually during start and stop.
