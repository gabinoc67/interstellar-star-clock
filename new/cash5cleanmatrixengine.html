Cash 5 Sorted Column Sequence & Co-Occurrence Engine

Each draw is sorted from lowest to highest. Column 1 is the lowest number, Column 5 is the highest. The engine studies frequency, recent activity, overdue pressure, one-step followers, two-step sequence matches, movement, exact cross-column companions, and column-specific ranges. It then creates 40 diverse tickets.

1. Paste Draw History

Paste newest-first or oldest-first. The parser accepts dates, tabs, commas, spaces, hyphens, prize amounts, and winner counts. It extracts the first five valid lottery numbers after the date and sorts them automatically.

Paste the draw history, then select Analyze Sorted Columns.

5. Generated 40-Ticket Matrix

Tickets are strictly ascending, contain no duplicate numbers, favor 2/3 or 3/2 odd-even balance, limit extreme low/high concentration, avoid four-number clones, and distribute candidate exposure across the 40 rows.

Statistical research tool only. Lottery drawings are designed to be random, and no sequence method can guarantee future winning numbers.
