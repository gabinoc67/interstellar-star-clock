Portfolio diagnostics

Broad-coverage section: the first 20 tickets are chosen from a large random candidate pool. They favor underused numbers, unused triples, moderate sums, and low overlap. Broad tickets may share no more than two numbers with another broad ticket.

Moderate-cluster section: the next 12 tickets are divided into four groups. Each group uses one unique two-number anchor pair, and each anchor pair appears in exactly three cluster tickets. The remaining three numbers vary across those tickets.

Bridge section: four tickets are built after the broad and cluster sections. Each bridge ticket combines one two-number pair from one selected ticket with a different two-number pair from another selected ticket, then adds a fifth balancing number. This deliberately reconnects combinations that are already represented elsewhere in the portfolio.

Pure-random section: the final four tickets are generated directly with browser cryptographic randomness. They are not scored for sums, parity, zones, or past drawings. Duplicate tickets, four-number overlaps, and pair-limit violations are still rejected.

Portfolio protection: no final ticket is duplicated, no two final tickets may share more than three numbers, and no pair may appear more than three times. These controls diversify the purchase but do not predict winning numbers.
