Texas Cash Five 40-Ticket Generator

Generates 40 cryptographic Quick Pick-style tickets from numbers 1–35, then selects a diversified final set with controlled overlap.

This program uses browser cryptographic randomness, which is suitable for Quick Pick-style number selection. It cannot duplicate or verify the proprietary random system inside an official lottery terminal. Quick Picks do not know the winning numbers; they win frequently because many players buy them. Every valid combination has the same jackpot chance.

How the fixed method works: the program creates up to 100,000 unique cryptographic combinations. It then selects 40 tickets while rewarding underused numbers and previously unused pairs. A large random component remains in the selection so the result stays closer to Quick Pick behavior than a heavily filtered prediction system.

No past-draw weighting: previous results are not used. This avoids treating hot, cold, or overdue numbers as though they have a better chance in the next independent drawing.

Overlap control: no two final tickets may share more than three numbers. This does not improve the probability of an individual ticket, but it prevents purchasing nearly identical rows.
