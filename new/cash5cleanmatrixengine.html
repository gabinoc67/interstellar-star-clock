Texas Cash Five Generator

Generates diversified five-number combinations from 1 through 35 using cryptographic randomness and balanced formation filters.

Every valid five-number combination has the same mathematical chance of winning. This program organizes and diversifies selections; it cannot know or predict the future drawing.
Select your settings and press Generate Options.
Balanced mode: generally favors two odd and three even numbers, or three odd and two even numbers. It also distributes numbers across low, middle, and high ranges, limits long consecutive sequences, keeps sums within your selected range, and avoids near-duplicate tickets.

Pure Quick Pick: selects five unique numbers using crypto.getRandomValues() without formation filters.

Mixed mode: combines balanced selections with pure random selections so that not every ticket follows the same formation.
