RESEARCH DISCLAIMER
Conceptual show simulator only. The Earth-to-Mars and Mars-to-Earth values are simplified demonstration travel times based on the existing PETSHRE model, not operational flight predictions. The spacecraft mass is fixed at 150,000 kg and the displayed mission midpoint speed is 188 km/s. Momentum and kinetic energy use standard mechanics. Fission and fusion readings are illustrative power-flow channels; they do not prove that a real spacecraft of this mass could reach the displayed velocity using the shown reactor power, propellant, cooling, shielding, or conversion efficiency. The 0.75 curvature factor is hypothetical and is not evidence of artificial gravity, a wormhole, warp propulsion, or demonstrated spacetime compression. Mission energy is represented as continuously generated, converted and released, not stored as the total mission energy. Conservation of energy and momentum remain required.
